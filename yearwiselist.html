Timeline

Year-wise Listing of Viruses

1918
Influenza (Flu)

First identified as a global pandemic in 1918; the virus was isolated in the 1930s.

1976
Ebola Virus Disease

First identified near the Ebola River in what is now the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

1981
HIV

Recognised as a new disease in 1981; the virus was identified in 1983.

2019
Coronavirus (SARS-CoV-2)

First detected in late 2019 in Wuhan, China; declared a pandemic in March 2020.
