HIV (Human Immunodeficiency Virus)

HIV is a virus that attacks the body's immune system, the defence that normally fights off infection. If left untreated it can lead to AIDS, the most advanced stage of the infection. Thanks to modern treatment, HIV has gone from a near-certain death sentence to a manageable, lifelong condition, but it remains a serious global health challenge.
