MEET THE RESEARCHER

Vertex System is led by Dr. Sarah Mitchell, a virologist with over twelve years of research experience in infectious diseases. She holds a PhD in Molecular Virology from the University of Edinburgh and has worked with public health teams on outbreak response and vaccine research. Her goal with Vertex System is to translate complex virology into clear, accurate language anyone can understand.

Every article is grounded in trusted sources such as the World Health Organization and the CDC, then reviewed for accuracy before publishing. Science without the jargon, and facts without the fear.

Our Story

Why We Built This?

Viruses shape our world far more than most people realise, yet they remain badly misunderstood, feared in some places and dismissed in others. We started this platform to close that gap. The goal was simple: explain what viruses actually are, how they work, and why they matter, in language anyone can follow without a science degree. What makes our approach different is that we refuse to choose between accurate and accessible. Most sources are either too clinical to read or too shallow to trust. We pair real virology with clear writing and honest design, so understanding a virus no longer means decoding a textbook. Knowledge, not fear, is what keeps people safe, and that belief drives everything we publish.
